# Curios

With [Curios API](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/curios) installed, you get a Logistics Wrench slot. Wear your wrench there and it no longer needs a hotbar slot.

## Using the Worn Wrench

Press **K** to switch the worn wrench on or off. The key can be changed in Controls.

While it is on, everything works as if the wrench were in your hand: the mode wheel, wrench settings, the HUD, seeing hidden nodes, and placing, removing, copying and pasting nodes.

Your hand item stays where it is. Right-clicking only goes to the wrench when the wrench has something to do. Anywhere else your held item works as normal, so you can keep building and opening chests. In Mass Placement mode every right-click goes to the wrench.

If you hold a real wrench, that wrench is used instead.

A worn wrench that is switched off still counts as a wrench in your inventory for its storage link. The Computer does not pick up a worn wrench, so keep one in your inventory for that.
